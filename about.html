About Us

Learn more about Pet Age Calculator and our mission

Our Story

Pet Age Calculator was created with a simple mission: to help pet owners better understand their animal companions. We believe that knowing your pet's true age in human years is more than just a fun fact - it helps you provide the best possible care at every stage of their life. From dogs and cats to cows, horses, rabbits, and parrots, we cover the pets you love.

Why We Built This Tool

Most pet owners still use outdated rules to calculate their pet's age. However, modern veterinary science has shown that these methods are highly inaccurate. Animals age much faster in their first two years, and different species and breeds have dramatically different lifespans.

We built this calculator using scientifically-backed formulas:

  • AVMA Simple Method - Recommended by the American Veterinary Medical Association
  • UC San Diego Scientific Formula - Based on DNA methylation research
  • Breed-Adjusted Method - Our most accurate formula that accounts for breed-specific lifespans

Our Mission

We aim to provide pet owners with accurate, easy-to-use tools that help them make informed decisions about their pet's health and wellbeing. Whether you're wondering if your dog is entering their senior years, curious about your parrot's life stage, or checking your cow's age, our calculator gives you the answer backed by science.
