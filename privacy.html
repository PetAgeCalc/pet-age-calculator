Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here is how we handle your data.

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Pet Age Calculator ("we", "our", "us"), accessible at petagecalc.in, collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

Our pet age calculator runs entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit the pet details (breed, age, weight) you enter into the calculator - these calculations happen locally on your device and are never sent to our servers.

When you use our Contact Us form, we collect the name, email address, and message you voluntarily provide, solely to respond to your inquiry.

Cookies & Tracking

  • We may use cookies to remember basic site preferences and improve user experience
  • We may use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use our site
  • You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time

Affiliate Links

Some links on this website are affiliate links (including Amazon Associates links). If you click these links and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This helps us keep the calculator free for everyone.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any information submitted through our contact form. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us
